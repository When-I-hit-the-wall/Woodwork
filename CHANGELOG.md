# Changelog

## Unreleased — 0.1.0

- The repository, its licence (GPL-3.0, as Woodcore's engine it derives from) and the founding specification.
- The engine taken from Woodcore (`woodwork/lib/`), cut to what a sheet needs; `#domain` hands its settings to its lines (norm, positions, display, `units:`, `preset:`), prints no heading; `norm-position: "right"`; keys of several indices print as the norm writes them (N t,d).
- `#material`: a strength class of SIA 265 Tabelle 8 or 9, or of the office's `my-materials/`, and its design values η_w · η_t · f_d.
- `woodwork/data/`: 57 tables of SIA 265, SIA 265/1, SIA 260 and SN EN 14592, copied from Woodcore.
- `tools/generate.py`: the three language trees of `sheets/` from one source.
- The 18 checks of phase 1, standard and detailed, in fr, de and en (108 sheets): tension (parallel, perpendicular, at an angle), compression (parallel, perpendicular, at an angle), bending (parallel, biaxial, at an angle, with tension, with compression), shear (4.2.5.1, shear-off 4.2.6, with a stress perpendicular to the grain 4.2.7.1), buckling (4.2.8), lateral torsional buckling (4.2.9, with compression, with tension). Each one's utilisation equals Woodcore's member check for the same inputs.
- `#var(…, from:, path:)` reads a constant of `woodwork/data/`; `#eq(…, cases:)` computes by cases; a power's exponent may be a variable; `#material` also gives f_c,0,k, η_w,E and E_0,05 (Anhang B).
- The templates of form (`woodwork/forms/`: `neutral`, `compact`, `bfh`, each `#show: <name>`), the title pages `titre-bfh` and `titre-neutre`, and Woodcore's three documents as skeletons in three languages (`templates/`); `#show-norm-list`.
- The form `bfh` sets a table written straight into a figure in the BFH table style: title above and centred, grey-blue header, striped rows, the rows of `table.footer` as totals with a yellow mark, the legend under the table (spec 2026-11-05-style-tableaux-bfh).
- Numbers print their thousands set apart by an apostrophe: 28'000 mm² (the engineer's choice).
- The actions in Excel (`actions/fr/`), Woodcore's tabs «Neige», «Vent» and «Poids propre et surcharge» carried over before Woodcore is stopped: `neige.xlsx`, `vent.xlsx`, `poids-propre-et-surcharge.xlsx`, generated by `tools/build_actions.py` from `woodwork/data/sia261/` (SIA 261:2020, its 43 files copied from Woodcore, the clauses' text left out) and `source/actions/` (the materials and Lignum's eight build-ups); `tools/check_actions.py` finds them equal to Woodcore on 281 cases of snow and wind and the eight build-ups.
- `vent.xlsx` takes one sheet per table of Annexe C, each with three axonometric views of its building, its zones coloured and named (drawn from Woodcore's 3D model by `tools/render_wind_views.py`), the table itself and every direction's q_k; «Sommaire» links them.
- The export of a case of `actions/` to Typst (`tools/actions_typ.py`, a window, or `actions/exporter-typ.pyw`): a calculation file of `#var` and `#eq` that prints nothing, and three presentation files (résumé, standard, détaillé) that call it and recall its values; `check_actions.py` holds what Typst computes to the workbook.
- The content template «Simple Report» in three languages (`templates/fr/rapport-simple/`, `de/einfacher-bericht/`, `en/simple-report/`): a title block on the first page, then «1 Section» and «2 Annexes», no table of contents, no bibliography; the form `bfh` takes `chapter-break: false` for it.
- The user's Claude skill `woodwork-report` (`skill/`), installed into `~/.claude/skills/` by `python tools/install_skill.py`: the engine, the sheets and the templates it carries, a catalogue of the checks, the commands' reference, the method to write a report with Woodwork's sheets or free calculations. `--zip <file>` writes it into a zip to upload to claude.ai (Plugins > Skills).
- `#calc-defaults` names each of its arguments in its signature (`display`, … `presets`, `scope`), each documented, so an editor such as Tinymist lists them as it lists `#domain`'s; what it accepts and refuses is unchanged, an unknown argument being refused by Typst itself.
- `#calc-defaults(presets: "block-left")`: one argument for `presets: "block"`, `label-position: "left"`, `label-width: 35%`, `level: 3` and `scope: "domain"` (the engineer, 23.11.2026); an argument written beside it wins. `scope:` is now `auto` in the signature, `"next"` unless a bundle names another. The bundles live in `woodwork/lib/display-presets.json` (1.4).
- The skill `woodwork-report` teaches the house style of the engineer's reports (23.11.2026): a new report is a project folder (`StatischerBericht.typ`, `000-Typst-Config/{config,commands}.typ`, `.woodwork/`, `Sections/`, one calculation per file under `Einwirkungen/` and `Tragwerksanalyse/`), each domain opened by `#calc-defaults(presets: "block-left")`, one argument per line with section banners, every table a figure «Tabelle n: Name» in the BFH style.
- In a formula, an exponent written right after a literal's unit is the unit's: `10 "m"^2` is 10 m², no longer (10 m)² = 100 m², and `2 "kN/m"^2` is 2 kN/m²; `(10 "m")^2` still squares the quantity, and a caret inside the quotes (`10 "m^2"`) prints as ² (the engineer, 26.11.2026).
- The skill `woodwork-report` gives the project's `commands.typ` the engineer's `#line-text` (a word on the grid of the calculation lines, with its citation) and `#ref-calc` (a link «siehe Abschnitt n, S. p» to where a value is set), and makes a rule of citing, with `#ref-calc`, the source of every value read from another domain (the engineer, 26.11.2026).
- The development tooling: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/`, the skills (`requirements`, `big-feature`, `small-feature`, `architecture-review`, `implementation`, `verify-requirements`, `finish`, `issue`, `typst-sheet`, and Tinymist's `typst-writing-document`), the agents, and the test runner.
